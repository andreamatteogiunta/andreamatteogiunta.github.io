Stata lecturer for the Labor Economics course at Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore during the Fall 2024 semester.

Role
======

Taught the Stata sessions accompanying the course, guiding students through the empirical side of labor economics with hands-on work in Stata.

Topics
======

* Data management and cleaning in Stata
* Descriptive analysis of labor market data
* Regression analysis applied to labor economics questions
