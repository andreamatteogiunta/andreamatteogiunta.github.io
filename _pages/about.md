Welcome!

I am a PhD student in Economics and Finance at [Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore](https://www.unicatt.it/) in Milan.

My research studies low-pay dynamics, inequality, and intergenerational mobility. I am increasingly interested in development economics and the dynamics of wealth.

I hold a M.Sc. in Economics from Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore, complemented by a semester at [UC Berkeley](https://www.berkeley.edu/), and an BBA from Università degli [Studi di Firenze](https://www.unifi.it/it).
